## Project Summary/Abstract

Empowered Disease Management Health Services (EDMHS.org) proposes Inner Combat Training (ICT) for youth aged 10-24 in ZIP codes 43607, 43610, 43620. Free youth martial arts and behavioral wellness program in Toledo pairing traditional martial arts (Chung Do Kwan Tae Kwon Do, Shorin-Ryu karate, Dragon Style Kung Fu, Qi Gong breathing) with structured social-emotional learning, taught in small, stable cohorts. Instruction is keyed to developmental stage (Erikson, Piaget) rather than age alone.

### Approach
Free youth martial arts and behavioral wellness program in Toledo pairing traditional martial arts (Chung Do Kwan Tae Kwon Do, Shorin-Ryu karate, Dragon Style Kung Fu, Qi Gong breathing) with structured social-emotional learning, taught in small, stable cohorts. Instruction is keyed to developmental stage (Erikson, Piaget) rather than age alone.

- Ages 10-11: Industry vs. inferiority; Concrete operational. Focus: Frustration tolerance and persistence.
- Ages 12-14: Identity vs. role confusion (onset); Concrete to formal. Focus: Peer reactivity and self-consciousness.
- Ages 15-17: Identity vs. role confusion; Formal operational. Focus: Anger pathways, rumination; teaching younger students.
- Ages 18-24: Intimacy vs. isolation; Formal/post-formal. Focus: Paid peer mentor and assistant instructor roles.

Evidence base: (Zivin G, et al. Adolescence. 2001;36(143):443-459.) (Lakes KD, Hoyt WT. J Appl Dev Psychol. 2004;25(3):283-302.) (Harwood A, Lavidor M, Rassovsky Y. Aggress Violent Behav. 2017;34:96-101.) (Heller SB, et al. Q J Econ. 2017;132(1):1-54.)
