About

Physician-led. Community-rooted.

EDMHS.org grew out of Inner Combat Training, a community project with Soul City Wrestling and Boxing Clubs serving the neighborhoods where the Neighborhood Health Association was founded.

Founder

Darrell A. Hall, MD, MSCIS is a residency-trained family physician with fourteen years of community practice, an MS in Computer Information Systems from Boston University, and certification as a Professional Data Scientist. He served as a U.S. Air Force communications-electronics officer in Europe during the move to packet-switched defense networks, and has trained in martial arts for fifty years.
